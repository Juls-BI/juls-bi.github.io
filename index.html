About me

15+ years turning data into insight

Multi-award-winning Chartered IT Professional with over 15 years of experience in data and reporting across multiple industries. Microsoft Certified Power BI Data Analyst with a Professional Certificate in User Interface Design, specialising in end-to-end Power BI development, data modelling, DAX and report design.

Combines strong technical expertise with a focus on creating intuitive, accessible reporting experiences, translating complex business requirements into scalable semantic models and reports that help people understand their data and make confident decisions. Accessibility and inclusive design are central to this work, with WCAG guidance applied to practical data visualisation and reporting.

Alongside professional work, a recognised international speaker on Power BI, data visualisation and digital accessibility, as well as a registered STEM Ambassador and trustee for Technology Books for Children.
